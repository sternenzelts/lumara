# A-grade ability kits (2 each: skill + ultimate)
All names original. Shown on character pages at launch; playable in Phase 2.

## Sollene
- **Lunar Benediction** (Skill): She raises her crescent staff; soft silver moonlight falls on nearby allies and tiny stars drift around them.
- **Full Moon Vigil** (Ultimate): She kneels in prayer; a giant full moon rises behind her and bathes the whole field in calm silver light.

## Wren
- **Gale Volley** (Skill): She fires three quick arrows that curve on spiraling green wind currents.
- **Skybreaker Flight** (Ultimate): She leaps high, flips mid-air, and looses a single giant arrow that bursts into a tornado of leaves.

## Rook
- **Quickdraw Spark** (Skill): A blur-fast shot trailing amber lightning; the revolver spins back into his hand.
- **Thunderhead Showdown** (Ultimate): He tips back his coat, fires six shots into the sky, and six bolts of amber lightning strike down around him.

## Calla
- **Venom Tincture** (Skill): She tosses a glowing emerald flask; it shatters into a lingering cloud of bubbling poison.
- **Grand Alchemy** (Ultimate): A huge violet transmutation circle appears; flasks orbit her and explode together in a burst of emerald smoke.

## Kairo
- **Riptide Cut** (Skill): A single calm draw of his katana sends a crescent wave slicing forward.
- **Tidal Requiem** (Ultimate): He sheathes his blade slowly; a towering wave rises behind him and crashes down as the sheath clicks shut.

## Dax
- **Blazing Knuckle** (Skill): A fast flaming uppercut that launches a spiral of fire.
- **Inferno Rush** (Ultimate): A storm of fire-wrapped punches ending in a huge flaming fist that explodes into embers.
