# Azrenth reveal assets

The approved source is `reveal-demos/azrenth/` in the project root. The castle, Sever painting, throne painting, and Jirasd cutout are WebP quality 80 conversions of the supplied PNGs. The three paintings retain their 1672 × 941 dimensions; the rift coordinates in the reveal use this size. `venuzdonoa.webp` and the existing `azrenth_cutout.webp` retain their supplied dimensions.

The five `vo_*.mp3` clips, `theme.mp3`, `sfx_slash.mp3`, and `sfx_shatter.mp3` are copied from the approved demo. Shared pull audio and crystal art are referenced from their existing game locations.
