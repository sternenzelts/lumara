# Lucien reveal assets

The terrace, chant, three seals, vow, lunge, storm, four reveal voice clips, and 60-second theme were copied from `reveal-demos/lucien/`, the approved demo. The cutout and splash come from `characters/lucien/`. All copied images are WebP; the six scene images retain their 1672 × 941 source dimensions so the demo's measured effect points stay aligned.

The reveal reuses the existing shared pull assets, Seren's identical S+ tell, and Azrenth's identical slash and shatter effects. `thunder_sovereign.webp` and the unused Sanctuary voice set are not bundled.
