# Landscape reveal art

The original Ayaka, Mahesvara, and Keira paintings were supplied by Jay as 1672 x 941 PNGs. The game now bundles WebP quality 80 conversions at the same dimensions to keep the standalone build below 64 MB. Their original PNGs remain in the project source archive.

These files are used by the approved full-scene reveals. The existing cutout poses, audio, and wish-banner files are separate assets.
