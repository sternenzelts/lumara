# Upgrades implemented in interface

Based on 2026-09-27-upgrades-plan.md. Development stays in interface; no reverse copy from gacha-retro is performed.

## Economy

Starting Starlight is 1200; a paid banner wish costs 200. Attendance, votes cast and fulfilled shared Vows add Starlight. Voyage pulls are free. Rates are 0.5% S++, 3% S+ by default, with RNG injected for tests. Shared opening/banner pity guarantees S+ or better within 30 and S++ within 100; exchanges do not advance pity. Duplicates award configured Stardust; exchanges deduct their grade cost. The local backend validates funds inside its mutation lock, so a stale tab cannot spend a seventh starting-balance wish. Legacy settings gain start and migrate the old default cost to 200 without deleting progress.

## Tutorial

All new players see the full opening, including during a live Voyage. Named players with an unseen story see the story. Skip jumps to call, and Go to the Banner marks the story seen. Named/story-seen players with no owned companions are gated to the Banner from game screens; only Make a wish is active. The real RNG result enters the same shared reveal system. Continue or Close after the awarded reveal selects that companion, returns to Sanctuary and plays the recorded Seren grade line. The balance is then 1000. Title is exempt; existing companions bypass the tutorial. The companion appears as the HUD portrait and remains in the collection; its roaming Sanctuary rendering is still removed.

## Game UI

Dark translucent glass and thin gold frames cover panels and dialogs. Angled gold main actions have sweep, press and focus states; secondary actions use dark glass. Voyage is one large compass emblem with sprint and party count; a live session pulses and says Your party is waiting. Wallet + buttons open Wishes and Exchange. Twelve lamp pips display capped fulfilled-Vow progress. Seren has a gold name tab and speech pointer. Phone rename dialog is centered and traps/restores focus. Opening respects saved motion pause and reduced motion.

## Audio and assets

Every reveal uses the shared standard crystal pull, with grade-specific tells. The supplied Ayaka sequence, complete voices, sound levels and frozen Waltz remain intact. Ayaka and Mahesvara crossfade to their supplied character themes; no other character theme files exist in the supplied pack, so other characters currently use the shared pull music before the visual reveal. Unused duplicated demo audio was removed to retain the 64 MB build limit; the original art/ayaka voice and Waltz copies remain. Title and Sanctuary music retain their route restrictions and independent controls. No new artwork was generated.
