# Ayaka — S++ (Ice / Time)

**Source / inspiration:** *Wistoria: Wand and Sword* — Elfaria Albis Serfort (prodigy ice mage, youngest
top-rank mage, created the most spells; perfect public image, secretly "the embodiment of sloth").
Archetype only; not a copy. (Name note: "Ayaka" is also Genshin Impact's ice character — Jay's choice.)
**Taken from the archetype:** genius ice mage, effortless power, secret laziness, blue-white hair.
**Ours:** pale aqua/silver palette; **power = slowing time, and at full power stopping it**
(she can pause the world to take a nap); lounging-on-an-ice-throne pose.
