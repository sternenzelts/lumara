# Suvara - S+ (Radiant Light)

Suvara is the young ruler of Lianzhou, bound to the light dragon Yulan. Her current art shows the two seated together, with her sword across her lap.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `suvara_splash_v3.webp` | Collection, details, website, reveal card |
| `suvara_cutout_v2.webp` | Sanctuary, party, portraits, website |
| `suvara_splash_v1.webp`, `suvara_splash_v2.webp`, `suvara_cutout.webp` | Superseded source art |

The approved reveal is in `reveal-demos/suvara/`. The game port uses the dusk palace, arrival, bond, awakening triptych, Sentinel's Descent, and final splash. The Japanese voice recordings are in `voice/ja/`; English captions are in `voice-script.md`. The dedicated Descent sound is `audio/suvara_sfx_descent.mp3`.
