# Ashvane - S+ (Earth / Battle)

Ashvane is a retired battle mage with dry humor and a flawless spear combo. His current artwork shows the spear across his shoulder at sunset.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `ashvane_splash_v2.webp` | Collection, details, website, reveal card |
| `ashvane_cutout_v2.webp` | Sanctuary, party, portraits, website |
| `ashvane_splash_v1.webp`, `ashvane_cutout.webp` | Superseded source art |

The approved reveal is in `reveal-demos/ashvane/`. The game port uses the dusk battlefield, arrival, serious stance, combo triptych, Autumn's End, and final splash. Japanese voice recordings are in `voice/ja/`; English captions are in `voice-script.md`. The selected theme is `audio/ashvane_theme_tetsu_no_ori.mp3`.
