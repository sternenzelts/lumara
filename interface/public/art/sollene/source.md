# Sollene — A (Moonlight)

**Source / inspiration:** *Wuthering Waves* — Phoebe (gentle, devout Acolyte; long wavy blonde hair,
wide-brimmed white hat, purple eyes, white dress, staff; Spectro). Archetype only; not a copy.
**Taken from the archetype:** gentle devout priestess, kind nature, signature wide-brimmed hat,
long wavy blonde hair, violet eyes, staff.
**Ours:** moved to a **moonlight** theme (white, deep navy, silver) so she doesn't blend with Seren and
Suvara's white-and-gold; crescent-moon staff; kneeling prayer pose (the only kneeling character);
moonlit cathedral garden.
